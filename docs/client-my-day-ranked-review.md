## Handover and cover dependency

The old handover page only drafted text, and Cover duties was a placeholder. The requested waiting/cover sections need a real state. Add a bounded explicit workflow: owner saves a handover with notes, dates, named cover person and designated department manager/company administrator reviewer; owner submits; only the designated currently authorized reviewer approves/requests changes. Owner may cancel. Reviewer cannot see an unsubmitted draft. Cover person sees only approved handover notes and a read-only task metadata projection while cover is active; no original source, private task notes, private drafts or broad role access is granted. No automatic publishing, outbound email or company invitation is performed. A lone company owner needs another authorized reviewer before submitting a handover.
