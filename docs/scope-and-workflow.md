# First release and workflow

## Build sequence

1. Foundation: project layout, tokens, shared UI, navigation and checks.
2. Authentication: signup, confirmation, login and password recovery.
3. Company setup: invitations, departments, small team mode and role policies.
4. Role records, decisions, actions and My day.
5. Capture: notes, supported files, company email forwarding, AI review and source links.
6. Planned handovers: draft, submit, approve or request changes, publish, acknowledge, work and take back.
7. Security, mobile verification, deployment and a real team pilot.

Wave two and Stage 2 follow pilot validation. Emergency handovers, successor onboarding, Ask Vimbre, advanced reports, direct mailbox connections and billing are not part of the scaffold.

## Remaining product decisions

Confirm small team approval behaviour, who can initiate a handover, exact cover access windows and edit rights, open task handling at return, timezone, providers and spending caps, attachment limits and retention. No commercial dates or fees are committed by this repository.
