# Capture final specification — 26 October 2026

Authority: Donato's three screenshots image(20261026-180738).png, image(20261026-180819).png and image(20261026-180834).png. This supersedes previous Capture layout, picker and Watch instructions. Preserve My Day and role/company privacy. Watch is explicit opt-in and creates drafts only; it never confirms work.

## Presentation

One title, one short line and one card. Title: Capture the conversation. Line: Pick or paste a conversation. Vimbre drafts the tasks and decisions. You check them first.

Source chips: Paste, Gmail, Drive and other connected tools. Forward email and Upload last; Add a tool is a small link to Settings. Connect/Disconnect/Reconnect live in Settings. Expired source chips offer Reconnect Gmail (or the real tool). Use real logos, not letters. Steps: Add, Check, Saved. One blue primary action per screen; remaining controls quiet outline or text.

Paste label: Paste the conversation. Placeholder: Example: We agreed to send the revised quote by Friday. Amara will handle it. Optional date/title/label under Title and label (optional). Show character count only near the limit. Draft tasks and decisions shows allowance beside it. Add manually replaces Add draft manually. Remove Improve text; code removes signatures/old replies, with a short note.

Your captures is a flat list with filters All, Needs review, Saved, No actions, Archived. Rows show real source logo, title, date and correctly pluralised task/decision counts. States: Needs review, Saved, No actions found, Could not read (reason and Retry). Never collapse distinct outcomes into Failed.

## Source pickers

Use provider-native search and opaque provider pagination; 20 metadata results per page and load more on scroll. Never download a full mailbox. Fetch bodies only for selected conversations. Cache result lists five minutes.

Gmail/Outlook: sender/subject/words search; Today, Last 7 days (default), Last 30 days, custom range; From, Folder/label, Has attachment, Unread, Likely needs action. Not captured yet on by default. Group conversations, not individual emails. Rows show sender, subject, one-line preview, date, checkbox; Captured links to the result. Cleaned-text preview at right; sticky selected/allowance/draft bar. Likely needs action uses local request/deadline/question rules, never AI.

Drive: file-name search; Docs/Sheets/PDF type; modified date/owner; folder browsing. Slack/Teams: channel or direct message, date/person/words, one or several threads. Trello: board/list/card and recent-activity filter. Paste accepts conversation, optional date and label. Forward email uses a personal address and only verified senders; matches arrive as drafts in My Day To review. Upload supports PDF/Word/text/transcripts.

## Watch

Explicit Watch this place on connected sources (Gmail label, Slack channel, Trello board etc). Conditions: mention me, chosen people, chosen words. Frequency, daily cap and pause. Matches become private drafts in To review. Each conversation uses the shared AI allowance; pause visibly when exhausted. No work is confirmed automatically.

## Check and Saved

Draft type Task/Decision, title, owner, due date, reminder. Highlight the source sentence and show sender/date. Flag missing owner/date only. Inline Edit, Accept, Dismiss; Accept all. Merge duplicate proposals, flag matches to existing tasks. Saved: correctly pluralised task/decision result with Open My day and Capture another. Keep legacy summaries/contacts readable.

## Rules and data

One conversation is one AI draft, show cost before drafting; queue batches up to ten. Cache AI results by cleaned-text fingerprint, source-ID and fingerprint duplicate protection. Each person connects their own account; admins choose allowed tools. Minimum read-only consent for Capture. Keep separately approved, explicit My Day source updates working.

Privacy line: Vimbre reads only what you pick, or what matches your Watch rules.

Single column, generous spacing, 56 px picker rows; arrows move, Space selects, Enter previews, Command/Ctrl+Enter drafts. Store source type/account/reference, result state, error reason, task/decision counts, archive/delete time and Watch source/place/filters/frequency/daily-cap/enabled fields. Preserve immutable confirmed sources, cached AI reuse, assignment acceptance, scoped ownership and company boundaries.
