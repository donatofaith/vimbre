SCOPE AND CAPACITY
[ ] Coverage dates and responsibilities agreed
[ ] Recipient capacity checked
[ ] Work to pause agreed with stakeholders

WORK AND RISK
[ ] Critical deadlines have an owner
[ ] Customer / supplier commitments recorded
[ ] Recurring duties have instructions and next dates
[ ] Decision reasons and approval limits documented
[ ] Known risks have mitigation and escalation steps

ACCESS AND RECORDS
[ ] Recipient can open the current files
[ ] Approved access requests completed
[ ] Sensitive information shared only where needed
[ ] No passwords or recovery codes in handover notes

ACCEPTANCE
[ ] Recipient walked through critical items
[ ] Open question / owner / due date recorded
[ ] Required approvals completed
[ ] Relevant stakeholders informed
